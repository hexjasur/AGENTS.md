# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our standard

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Assume good intent, give specific feedback, and focus on the work. Welcome people of every background and experience level.

Not acceptable: harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, trolling, publishing others' private information, spam, or sexualized language or behavior directed at others.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone represents the project. Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions and may restrict participation for behavior that violates this code.

To report a conduct concern privately, use the GitHub [private reporting form](https://github.com/hexjasur/AGENTS.md/security/advisories/new) and mark it as a conduct report. Reports are handled confidentially.
